# Slices

- [Habbos](/slices/habbos.md) — A habbo stands in a room of the client opened from the site, and its look and motto, changed on the site or in housekeeping, change in the client at once, through the first service. **Active.**
- [Platform: reborn runs the whole hotel](/slices/platform.md) — The orchestrator reborn runs the installer, the site, housekeeping and the game server in one process, installs a new hotel live, stops in order and ships beside the four separate programs; every program reads settings from the store. **Active.**
- [The client in the browser](/slices/client.md) — A signed-in habbo opens the site's client page, where the R26 client runs on LibreShockwave; one program serves the client's files; and later the client runs in vanilla JavaScript. **Active.**
- [Assets out of the repository](/slices/assets.md) — Every Habbo file leaves the repository and its history, and a tool fetches them, pinned, into ignored folders the programs serve from disk.
- [Look and feel: Holo's look at today's sizes](/slices/look-and-feel.md) — The site matches HoloCMS where it drifted, and is redesigned at a 16px base with a wider layout that keeps Holo's look and feel.
- [Installer: a first article, optional content and more settings](/slices/installer.md) — A new hotel starts with a first news article, and its administrator chooses which content is seeded, reviews the catalogue and sets more of the hotel's settings while installing.
- [Economy](/slices/economy.md) — Housekeeping changes credits, tickets and film, the site shows them and spends them in vouchers and purchases, and the game server shows a player its new balance at once.
- [Club](/slices/club.md) — Club is bought on the site and in the client, its gifts are given, and the game shows a player its club at once.
- [Rooms](/slices/rooms.md) — Loaded rooms, with their habbos and furni, stay in the game's memory, and a change made to a room or its furni outside the game reaches it at once.
- [Navigator](/slices/navigator.md) — The navigator's categories and rooms, changed outside the game, reach it at once, and the site shows the rooms the navigator lists.
- [Messenger: friends and their chat](/slices/messenger.md) — Habbos keep a friends list, send and answer friend requests, search for habbos, follow friends into rooms and chat with friends who are online, and the site shows a habbo's friends.
- [Furnis, with the catalogue](/slices/furnis.md) — Housekeeping edits the catalogue and a habbo's furni, and the furni left out of S5 arrives: one-way doors, the fortune teller, limits for post-its and rollers, and the pages that wait for their features.
- [Presence and rooms on the site](/slices/presence-and-rooms.md) — The site and housekeeping show who is in the hotel and where: the online count and states, Online Users, My Rooms, recommended rooms and the first-login room picker.
- [Moderation](/slices/moderation.md) — Staff moderate the hotel from the client and from housekeeping: chat commands, the word filter, calls for help, bans and alerts that reach the game at once, and staff tools for groups.
- [Badges and achievements](/slices/badges-and-achievements.md) — The site shows a habbo's five worn badges, and habbos earn achievements.
- [Extras: the client's smaller features](/slices/extras.md) — The remaining casts with a protocol: the tutorial and new-user help, guides, polls, photos, the sound machine, the room dimmer, pets, the dynamic downloader and error reports, each with the catalogue pages that sell it.
- [Instant games](/slices/games.md) — Habbos create, join, watch and play the instant games of hh_ig, from the lobby to the arena, with the Game Hall of Fame.
- [r18, a second protocol](/slices/r18.md) — The game server also speaks the protocol of an older client, r18. **Parked.**
