# The R26 protocol by cast

One document per client cast, in the [format](/reference/client-r26.md#protocol-reference-format) of The R26 client,
each written by its task in [S1](/phases/s1-protocol-reference.md).

- [R26 fuse_client](/reference/r26/fuse_client.md) — How the R26 client opens its #info and #mus connections, frames packets both ways, encodes every value, and maps listeners and commands, from fuse_client's .ls scripts.
- [R26 hh_entry_init](/reference/r26/hh_entry_init.md) — How the R26 client logs in, from the handshake without encryption to the user object, and what it does with rights, badges, achievements, alerts, pings, latency tests, logouts and opening hours, from hh_entry_init's .ls scripts.
- [R26 hh_shared](/reference/r26/hh_shared.md) — How the R26 client sends calls for help, how staff take them up, answer them and moderate with the mod tool, and how it asks the server's date, shows the server's error reports and passes tracking codes on, from hh_shared's .ls scripts.
- [R26 hh_club](/reference/r26/hh_club.md) — How the R26 client asks for its club status after the login and shows it, buys club periods in the club window, and accepts or refuses the club's gifts, from hh_club's .ls scripts.
- [R26 hh_human](/reference/r26/hh_human.md) — How the R26 client reads the figure strings the server sends, from the figure data files it downloads, and the one command its figure system registers and never sends, from hh_human's .ls scripts.
- [R26 hh_navigator](/reference/r26/hh_navigator.md) — How the R26 client browses the navigator's public and private categories, lists its own rooms, searches and keeps favourites, reads and changes a room's settings, deletes a room, enters a room or is refused, and is forwarded, from hh_navigator's .ls scripts.
- [R26 hh_buffer](/reference/r26/hh_buffer.md) — How the R26 client holds back the updates of furniture whose graphics are still downloading and replays them through hh_room once they arrive, from hh_buffer's .ls scripts.
- [R26 hh_dynamic_downloader](/reference/r26/hh_dynamic_downloader.md) — How the R26 client asks the server, once, for the furniture aliases and revisions before it downloads a furniture class's cast, and which address it builds for the download, from hh_dynamic_downloader's .ls scripts.
- [R26 hh_roomdimmer](/reference/r26/hh_roomdimmer.md) — How the R26 client loads a room dimmer's presets, saves and applies one, turns the dimmer on and off, and fades the room to the state the dimmer's furniture carries, from hh_roomdimmer's .ls scripts.
- [R26 hh_poll](/reference/r26/hh_poll.md) — How the R26 client is offered a poll, accepts or refuses it, receives its questions and answers them one by one, from hh_poll's .ls scripts.
- [R26 hh_recycler](/reference/r26/hh_recycler.md) — How the R26 client loads the furniture recycler's rewards and status, recycles furniture from the hand, and takes or refuses the reward once the recycling is over, from hh_recycler's .ls scripts.
- [R26 hh_instant_messenger](/reference/r26/hh_instant_messenger.md) — How the R26 client chats with friends in the instant messenger, invites friends to the room it is in, and shows the server's errors, from hh_instant_messenger's .ls scripts.
- [R26 hh_kiosk_room](/reference/r26/hh_kiosk_room.md) — How the R26 client creates a room in the Roommatic kiosk, names it, sets its category, model and door, receives its number and goes in, from hh_kiosk_room's .ls scripts.
- [R26 hh_guide](/reference/r26/hh_guide.md) — How the R26 client's guide tool tells a guide whether it may help new users, lets the guide wait for invitations, and accepts or refuses a new user's invitation, from hh_guide's .ls scripts.
- [R26 hh_room_utils](/reference/r26/hh_room_utils.md) — How the R26 client's help dialogs check for a waiting call for help, delete it and send a new one, how a one-way door opens and shuts, and how room chat, whispers and shouts travel, from hh_room_utils' .ls scripts.
- [R26 hh_photo](/reference/r26/hh_photo.md) — How the R26 client takes a photo with the camera and stores it over #mus, shows a photo item by fetching its data over #mus, and keeps the film count, from hh_photo's .ls scripts.
- [R26 hh_tutorial](/reference/r26/hh_tutorial.md) — How the R26 client's tutorial asks for its preferences, configuration, topics and status and reports a finished topic, and how the new-user help shows its help items and invites guides to a new user's room, from hh_tutorial's .ls scripts.
- [R26 hh_friend_list](/reference/r26/hh_friend_list.md) — How the R26 client loads the friend list after the login, keeps it up to date, sends, accepts and declines friend requests, removes friends, searches for users, follows a friend and hears of new mail, from hh_friend_list's .ls scripts.
- [R26 hh_cat_code](/reference/r26/hh_cat_code.md) — How the R26 client's catalogue asks for its index and pages, which it reads as text, checks a pet's name, buys a product, as a gift or not, with the extra value its page gives it, and hears of the credit balance, from hh_cat_code's .ls scripts.
- [R26 hh_soundmachine](/reference/r26/hh_soundmachine.md) — How the R26 client plays a sound machine's or jukebox's play list, edits, saves, deletes and burns songs with the sound sets in the machine, keeps the play list, and loads disks into a jukebox, from hh_soundmachine's .ls scripts.
- [R26 hh_room](/reference/r26/hh_room.md) — How the R26 client enters a room, builds it from its height map, objects, items and users, keeps the users moving through status lines, and handles furniture, the hand, trading, teleports, rights, the doorbell, groups, tags, ratings, events and ignoring, from hh_room's .ls scripts.
- [R26 hh_ig](/reference/r26/hh_ig.md) — How the R26 client's instant games list, create, join, observe and start games in the lobby, load and enter the arena, and end a game with its scores, from hh_ig's .ls scripts; the game itself runs in casts the client downloads, which the repository does not hold.
- [R26 mus](/reference/r26/mus.md) — The bytes of the #mus connection under LibreShockwave, the WebSocket messages, the SMUS frame, the Logon frame the player sends, every serialized Lingo value, and the messages fuse_client and hh_photo exchange on it, each with a worked frame.
