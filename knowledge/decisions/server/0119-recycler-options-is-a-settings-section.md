# Decision

- Recycler Options is a form of housekeeping's settings, beside Game, with the recycler's four settings.
- The right `housekeeping.settings` opens it, and it is saved and logged like the other settings forms.

# Context

HoloCMS had Recycler Options in its Server menu, under General Configuration. Offered a settings section or a right of
its own, the user chose "A settings section (Recommended)": "It becomes one more form of housekeeping's settings,
'Recycler Options', beside 'Game', opened by the existing right housekeeping.settings and saved and logged like the
other settings forms. Example: whoever may change 'Game' settings may change the recycler's."
