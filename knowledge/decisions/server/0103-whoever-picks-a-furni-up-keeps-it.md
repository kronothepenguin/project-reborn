# Decision

- In a habbo room, the owner and the habbos with rights place furni from their own hands, and move and turn the room's
  furni.
- Only the room's owner, or a holder of `fuse_any_room_controller`, picks furni up, as the client shows its buttons.
- A furni picked up becomes its picker's: it goes to the picker's hand and belongs to the picker's user, whoever placed
  it.

# Context

Holo let habbos with rights place and move, and only the owner pick up, and gave what was picked up to the picker.
Offered that the furni goes back to whoever placed it, that whoever picks it up keeps it, or that only the owner places
furni, the user chose "Whoever picks it up keeps it, as Holo": "Holo's rule and the old hotel's: placing furni in
another habbo's room hands it over. Example: Bob places his Throne in Ana's room; when Ana picks it up it is hers, and a
moderator who picks it up keeps it too."

# Consequences

- s5-08 applies it.
- A deleted room's furni goes to its owner's hand by the same rule
  ([0059](/decisions/server/0059-a-deleted-rooms-furni-goes-to-its-owner.md)).
